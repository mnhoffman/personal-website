Currently

As of the last update (5/13/26), I’ve been paying particular attention to the relationship between my present, “zoomed in” self and my abstract, “big picture” self. This involves a lot of reflection on what I care about moment to moment, what I care about overall, and the systems I can create/implement to make smoother transitions between the two. I wrote a bit about this on substack (my first post!) but most of my thinking on this exists in recent conversations, scattered notes, or the abstract of my brain.

Speaking of, I’ve been inspired by Andy Matuschak and Michael Nielsen’s work on transformative tools for thought to vibecode a model of the ideas and tasks that bounce around in my brain sometimes— it’s going ok! I still don’t quite trust it with the actual mass of thoughts and tasks I swim through daily (which have a lot more to do with emails than the rest of this section lets on), but I’m feeling optimistic.
